# MVP Scope

## Must Have

- Login

- Tenant/company setup

- User profile

- Products and services

- Customers

- Cash sale

- Credit sale

- Customer payment

- Stock movement

- Customer balance

- Dashboard

- Mobile quick sale

- Mobile payment entry

- Limited offline transaction draft

- Manual sync now

## Should Have

- Low stock alert

- Receivables aging

- Simple gross profit estimate

- Basic subscription status

## Could Have Later

- Purchases

- Suppliers

- Stock counts

- Barcode scanning

- EBM integration

- Mobile Money automation

- Bank readiness score

## Not in MVP

- Full accounting

- Payroll

- Manufacturing

- HR

- Full offline ERP

- Automated bank integrations
