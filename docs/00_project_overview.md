# SME-OS Project Overview

## Mission

Build a simple, Rwanda-focused SME operating system that helps businesses manage sales, stock, customer debts, and business visibility.

## Target Users

- Retail shops

- Hardware stores

- Wholesalers

- Pharmacies

- Service businesses

- Future manufacturing businesses

## Founder Strategy

Start small with sales, stock, and customer debts.

Validate with real users.

Expand into accounting, integrations, credit readiness, services, and manufacturing later.

## Core Product Promise

Help SMEs answer:

1. What did I sell today?

2. What stock do I have?

3. Who owes me money?

4. Am I becoming more organized and bankable?
